How it works

A food brand without
a storefront.

Picky Eaters is a virtual food brand. There is no Picky Eaters dining room. The food comes from kitchens we partner with, and it comes to you.

1

We build the menu

Picky Eaters creates and owns the brand, the menu, the recipes and the way every plate is put together.

2

A partner kitchen cooks it

Restaurant kitchens we work with cook the Picky Eaters menu to our recipes, right alongside their own.

3

The apps bring it home

You order Picky Eaters through delivery apps, and they handle the last mile to your door.

We are not taking orders yet. Join the waitlist to hear first when we open.

How we start

  • 1. A tasting at your place
